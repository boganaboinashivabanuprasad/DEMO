Make Legal Notices Understandable — Instantly

Upload any notice (court/legal/FIR/policy). The system extracts text, explains it in simple language, highlights important dates, explains IPC sections, and suggests next actions for common people.

Language: Azure Translator
Logic: Scenario + IPC knowledge
